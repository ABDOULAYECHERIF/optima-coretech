AI Agents for Businesses

Answer every customer, 24/7, without hiring

  1. Home
  2. Services
  3. AI Agents for Businesses

OptimaCoreTech builds AI agents that work for your business around the clock. They answer questions, take bookings and orders, and capture leads on your website chat, WhatsApp or by phone, so you never lose a customer because nobody picked up.

What our AI agents do

  • Answer customer questions 24/7: opening hours, prices, services, availability, directions.
  • Take bookings and reservations and add them to your calendar or booking system.
  • Take orders for restaurants, takeaways and shops.
  • Capture and qualify leads, then notify your team instantly.
  • Speak several languages for an international customer base.
  • Hand over to a human whenever a conversation needs your team.

Built for real businesses

Our AI agents are especially effective for restaurants, hotels, medical and wellness practices, salons, and service businesses that receive many repetitive questions and booking requests.

How it works

  1. We learn your business: services, prices, policies and tone of voice.
  2. We train and connect the agent to your website, WhatsApp, phone line and tools.
  3. We test it thoroughly with real scenarios before going live.
  4. We launch and keep improving it based on real conversations.

Why an AI agent pays for itself

Every missed call or unanswered message after hours is a customer who may go elsewhere. An AI agent captures those customers, frees your staff from repetitive questions and never takes a day off.
