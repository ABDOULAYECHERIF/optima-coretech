OptimaCoreTech is a web design studio based in Antwerp. We design and build websites for businesses across Belgium: fast, mobile-friendly sites built to be found on Google and to turn visitors into enquiries, bookings and sales. More than 50 businesses trust us with their online presence.

What every website we build includes

  • Custom design built around your brand and your customers, not a generic template.
  • Mobile-first development that works perfectly on phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Speed: optimised images, clean code and modern hosting so pages load fast.
  • Technical SEO: page titles, meta descriptions, structured data, sitemap and Google Search Console setup.
  • Clear calls to action: call, book, request a quote or buy, on every page.
  • Domain, hosting and email setup handled for you.

Websites for every kind of business

We build business websites, online shops, booking websites, landing pages and website redesigns, for restaurants, medical practices, salons, trades, professional services and growing companies.

How we work

  1. Discovery call: we learn about your business, your customers and your goals.
  2. Proposal and quote: a clear plan, timeline and tailored price. No fixed packages that don't fit.

  4. Development and content: we build, fill and test your website.
  5. Launch: we go live and set up Google Search Console and analytics.
  6. After launch: optional monthly care plans for updates, backups and changes.
