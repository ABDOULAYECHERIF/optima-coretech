Do I Still Need a Website if I Have a Facebook Page?

Social media is great for reaching people who already know you. Here's why it isn't enough to be found by new customers, and what a website adds.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Do I Still Need a Website if I Have a Facebook Page?
Strategy2 October 20265 min read

Plenty of successful small businesses run entirely on a Facebook page or an Instagram account. It's free, it's familiar and your customers are already there. So is a website still worth it?

Short answer: yes, if you want new customers to find you. Here's why.

Social media is rented land

Your Facebook page doesn't belong to you. The platform decides who sees your posts, can change its rules overnight, and can suspend accounts with little warning. Organic reach for business pages is a small fraction of your followers. A website is the one place online that you fully own and control.

Google doesn't send people to your Facebook page

When someone searches "dentist near me" or "best Italian restaurant Antwerp", Google mostly shows websites and Google Business Profiles, not Facebook posts. Without a website, you are invisible to people actively searching for exactly what you offer. That is the most valuable traffic there is, because those people are ready to buy.

A website builds trust in seconds

Customers check you out before they call. A clean, professional website with your services, opening hours, real photos and reviews tells them you are established and serious. A business with only a social profile can look temporary, especially to older customers and to other businesses.

What a website does that social media can't

  • Appear in Google search for your services and your area.
  • Take bookings, orders and payments directly, without commission to a third-party platform.
  • Answer questions 24/7 with an AI assistant, so you don't lose leads after closing time.
  • Collect leads with contact and quote forms you own.
  • Show everything in one place: menu, prices, portfolio, team, FAQs.

The best setup: use both

This isn't website versus social media. Social media is where you stay in touch with people who already know you. Your website is where new customers find you and where everyone goes to take action. Link your social profiles to your website, and send every post's traffic to a page where people can book, call or buy.
