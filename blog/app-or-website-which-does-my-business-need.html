App or Website: Which Does Your Business Need?

A clear comparison of cost, reach and features to help you decide whether your business needs a mobile app, a website, or both.

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. App or Website: Which Does Your Business Need?
Apps2 October 20266 min read

"Should we build an app?" is a question we hear often, and sometimes the honest answer is "not yet". Apps and websites solve different problems. Here is how to decide what your business needs first.

The key difference

A website is how new customers find you. It works on every device, needs no installation and can be found on Google. A mobile app is how existing customers come back. It lives on their phone, can send notifications and offers a faster, richer experience for people who use your service often.

Website vs app at a glance

WebsiteMobile app
Found on GoogleYesNo (app stores only)
Works without installingYesNo
Push notificationsLimitedYes
Works offline / uses phone featuresLimitedYes
Typical cost to buildLowerHigher
Best forVisibility, trust, new customersLoyalty, repeat use, convenience

Start with a website if...

  • You need new customers to find you on Google.
  • Most of your customers visit or buy occasionally.
  • You don't have a professional online presence yet.

Build an app if...

  • Customers use your service often (weekly or daily).
  • You want a loyalty programme, ordering or bookings in one tap.
  • Push notifications would bring people back (offers, reminders, order updates).
  • You need phone features such as the camera, location or offline access.

The smart path for most businesses

For most small and medium businesses, the right order is: a strong website first, then an app once you have regular customers who would use it. Many businesses also combine both with an AI agent that serves customers on the website and in the app.
