Mobile App Development in Belgium

iOS and Android apps your customers will actually use

OptimaCoreTech designs and builds mobile apps for businesses in Antwerp and across Belgium. From booking and ordering apps to loyalty programmes and internal tools, we build iOS and Android apps that are fast, easy to use and built around your customers.

Apps we build

  • Booking and appointment apps for clinics, salons and service businesses.
  • Ordering and loyalty apps for restaurants, cafés and retail.
  • Internal business tools such as time tracking, field service and dashboards.
  • Customer portals connected to your existing systems.
  • MVPs for startups to test an idea quickly with real users.

What's included

  • UX and UI design tailored to your users.
  • iOS and Android development.
  • Admin dashboard and backend.
  • Push notifications, payments and integrations.
  • App Store and Google Play publishing.
  • Optional maintenance and updates after launch.

How we work

  1. Discovery: we define who the app is for and what it must do.
  2. Design: clickable prototypes you approve before development.
  3. Development: built in iterations, with regular demos.
  4. Testing and launch: thorough testing, then publishing to the app stores.
  5. Growth: improvements based on real user feedback.
